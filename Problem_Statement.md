# Agentic AI: The "Red Tape" Bureaucracy Navigator

## Problem Statement

Everyday citizens and small business owners waste weeks trying to navigate the maze of local government bureaucracy. Whether applying for a neighbourhood food vendor license, securing residential zoning permits, or accessing agricultural grants, the process is usually buried in confusing departmental websites, outdated PDF forms, and contradictory requirements that require a legal or administrative background to understand.

## Challenge

Build an autonomous "Red Tape" AI Agent that acts as a proactive legal-administrative assistant. The agent should take a simple, natural language goal (e.g., "I want to open a small bakery in my town") and independently research local regulations, fetch the correct forms, interview the user for missing details, and auto-fill the required paperwork for submission.

## Key Requirements

- A web-scraping and reasoning agent capable of navigating unstructured, text-heavy government or municipal portals to extract accurate, up-to-date compliance steps.
- An interactive memory system that allows the agent to conduct a conversational "interview" with the user, asking only for the specific personal or business details needed for the application.
- Document processing tools that allow the agent to map the collected information and autonomously populate official PDF forms or digital web portals.
- A transparency and review checkpoint where the agent presents the finalized, filled-out forms and a summary of the regulations to the user for final signature and submission.
