# Code of Conduct

Red Tape Navigator exists to help ordinary people deal with government paperwork. The way we work together should be just as respectful, clear and fair as the service we want to give.

## Our pledge

We want everyone who takes part in this project (team members, approved contributors, reviewers, testers and people who report problems) to have a harassment-free experience, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, religion, caste, language, appearance, or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people using or reviewing this project are not engineers or lawyers.
- Give and accept feedback with respect. Criticise the work, never the person.
- Use plain words. Explain terms, and welcome questions in English, Hindi or Bengali.
- Be honest about what is known and unknown, especially about laws, fees and deadlines. Never present a guess as an official fact.
- Respect privacy. Never share anyone's personal data, identity numbers (Aadhaar, PAN), documents or contact details, and never paste real ones into issues, pull requests or screenshots.
- Credit other people's work and follow the [licence](LICENSE).

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or demeaning comments of any kind.
- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Pressure to bypass the safeguards in this project, for example to submit to a government portal automatically, to get around a CAPTCHA, or to invent a rule that has no official source.
- Anything else a reasonable person would call unprofessional in a team setting.

## Where this applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, comments) and in any space where someone represents the project, such as a demo, a hackathon session or a pilot with shop owners.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, contact the repository owner through GitHub: **RajdipDhar2007**. Please keep sensitive details out of public comments. Reports are treated privately and handled fairly and promptly.

## What happens next

The owner and the team will look at each report, hear from the people involved, and decide a proportionate response. This can range from a private reminder, to removing a comment, to ending someone's access to the repository.
